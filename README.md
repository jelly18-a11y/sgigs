# SGigs 🚀

A zero-noise, privacy-first job board designed to eliminate social platform fatigue by routing users directly to official company career pages.

## Why SGigs?
Modern job hunting is cluttered with performative social feeds, forced profiles, and endless tracking. SGigs solves this by providing:
- **Zero Auth / No Login:** Open the site and view roles instantly. No profiles required.
- **Direct Links Only:** Apply straight on official company career portals (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, etc.) without middlemen.
- **Curated Focus:** High-intent roles across Product Management, Business Analytics, Business Development, and Project Management.

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend & UI:** Streamlit (Python)
- **Data Processing:** Python (`pandas`, JSON)
- **Hosting:** Streamlit Cloud
